Privacy Policy — Lunar Lords

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Lunar Lords ("the App") is developed by Michał Pełka, based in Poland ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

Data we collect

  • Account identifiers. When you play, the App creates an anonymous account. If you choose to link Google, Apple, or an email/password login, we store the resulting account identifier and — depending on the method and what you choose to share — your email address and/or name, via Firebase Authentication.
  • In-game profile. Your chosen nickname, selected nation, and game progress (buildings, army, resources, artifacts) are stored so your progress persists across devices and sessions.
  • Purchases. If you buy an in-game currency package, the purchase is processed by Apple/Google's payment system and verified through RevenueCat. We receive a confirmation record (product purchased, amount, and your account identifier) to credit your in-game currency — we do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can notify you of in-game events (e.g., being attacked by another player).
  • Leaderboard data. If you win the game, your nickname and score are published to a public Hall of Fame visible to other players.

Third parties we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions) — hosts your account and game data.
  • RevenueCat — processes and verifies in-app purchases made through Apple/Google.
  • Apple / Google Play — handle payment processing and, if you choose, Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In.
  • Expo (push notification service) — delivers push notifications to your device.

We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App (Account settings → Delete account). This permanently removes your game progress, profile, and linked identifiers from our servers. Some purchase records may be retained by Apple/Google/RevenueCat independently, per their own policies, for accounting and legal purposes.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under that age.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under GDPR to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the App, or by contacting us below.
