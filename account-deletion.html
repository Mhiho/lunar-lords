Account Deletion — Lunar Lords

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Lunar Lords is developed by Michał Pełka. This page explains how to delete your Lunar Lords account and what happens to your data when you do.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Lunar Lords app.
  2. Go to Account (from the Settings menu).
  3. Scroll to Account management and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by typing your nickname (or the confirmation word shown) when prompted.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. You must have an active internet connection to complete it, since your data is removed from our servers at the same time as your local device data.

What gets deleted

  • Your in-game profile: nickname, selected nation, game progress (buildings, army, resources, artifacts).
  • Your account identifiers (linked Google, Apple, and/or email/password credentials).
  • Your push notification token.
  • Your entry in the public Hall of Fame leaderboard, if you had one.

What may be retained

Purchase records may be retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat independently of your Lunar Lords account, per their own policies, for accounting and legal purposes (e.g. tax records, fraud prevention). We do not control or have access to delete these records — you'd need to contact Apple/Google directly for that.
