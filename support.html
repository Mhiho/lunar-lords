Common questions

I lost my progress

Lunar Lords starts you on an anonymous, guest account tied to this device only. If you uninstall the app, switch devices, or clear app data without first linking a Google, Apple, or email account (Settings → Account), your progress cannot be recovered. Link an account before switching devices to keep your progress safe.

I paid for silver/currency but didn't receive it

Purchases are usually credited within a few seconds. If it's been longer than that, make sure you're connected to the internet and reopen the app. If it's still missing, email us at the address above with your order/receipt confirmation from Apple or Google and the nickname or account you play on — we'll investigate.

How do I delete my account?

See our Account Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

I found a bug or want to suggest something

We'd love to hear about it — email us at the address above with as much detail as you can (what happened, what you expected, and your device/OS if it's a technical issue).
